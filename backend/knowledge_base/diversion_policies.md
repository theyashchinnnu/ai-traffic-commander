# Road Diversion & Traffic Management Policies

## 1. General Principles of Road Diversions
- **Objective**: To safely reroute traffic around road blockages, construction zones, or security perimeters while minimizing travel time delay and vehicle conflicts.
- **Advance Warning Requirements**:
  - Signage must be placed upstream to allow drivers to make diversion decisions before reaching the block.
  - **Highways**: First warning sign at 1000 meters, second at 500 meters, third at 200 meters.
  - **Urban Arterials**: Warning signs at 200 meters, 100 meters, and 50 meters.
- **Clearance and Dimensions**:
  - Diversion routes must be wide enough to accommodate the largest vehicle category permitted on the closed road.
  - Height clearance must be checked (minimum 4.5 meters for double-decker buses and freight containers).

## 2. Detour Route Selection Criteria
- **Capacity Analysis**:
  - The detour road must have sufficient capacity (number of lanes) to handle at least 60% of the diverted volume without causing gridlock.
  - Avoid diverting heavy vehicles through narrow residential streets or school zones.
- **Structural Integrity Check**:
  - Bridges and culverts along the detour route must be rated for the axle loads of diverted commercial vehicles.
- **Intersection Control**:
  - Major intersections along the detour route must have signal timing adjusted to prioritize the diverted flow.
  - Deploy traffic marshals at key turning points to guide traffic.

## 3. Contraflow Lane Operation Procedures
- **Definition**: Rerouting traffic from a closed carriageway to one or more lanes of the opposite carriageway.
- **Safety Barriers**:
  - Physical channelizing devices (cones, flexible posts, concrete barriers) must separate opposing traffic streams.
  - Speed limits must be reduced by at least 20 km/h in the contraflow zone.
- **Signage and Lighting**:
  - Retroreflective overhead arrows indicating active lanes (green arrow) and closed lanes (red cross).
  - High-intensity warning lights must be active 24/7 along the transition zone.

## 4. VIP Movement and Event Traffic Management
- **VIP Corridors**:
  - Temporary road closures for VIP transit must be restricted to a maximum duration of 10 minutes prior to passage.
  - Continuous communication between the motorcade coordinator and the central traffic control room is mandatory.
- **Event Traffic Control**:
  - Event organizers must submit a Traffic Management Plan (TMP) at least 30 days prior to major events (sports, concerts, rallies).
  - Designate specific park-and-ride shuttle pick-up points outside the event core perimeter.
- **Public Communication**:
  - Publish diversion plans in local media and update digital mapping providers (Google Maps, OpenStreetMap) at least 48 hours prior to planned events.

## 5. Construction Zone Safety Guidelines
- **Buffer Spaces**:
  - Establish a longitudinal buffer space between the transition taper and the active work area. No equipment or materials may be stored in this buffer.
- **Temporary Traffic Control Devices**:
  - High-visibility drums, cones, and barricades.
  - Variable Message Signs (VMS) updating motorists on lane closures and speed limits in real-time.
- **Night Work Illumination**:
  - Glare-free balloon lights or floodlights must fully illuminate the work zone and diversion entry point, ensuring drivers can easily distinguish the detour path.
